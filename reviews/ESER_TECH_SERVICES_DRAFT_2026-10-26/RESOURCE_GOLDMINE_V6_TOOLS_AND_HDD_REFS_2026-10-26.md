- **n8n:** The official n8n help-center page says the free self-hosted Community edition can be used by an agency that builds/runs workflows for clients without letting clients edit workflows; customer-configurable workflow editing and white-label hosting may require commercial licensing: https://support.n8n.io/article/can-i-use-your-license-for-my-use-case.

Headcount aggregation rules matter for agency/client collaborations: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq and https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing.
- **GSAP:** The official Webflow announcement says GSAP and previously paid plugins are free for users; this does not imply rights to resell the GSAP library itself: https://webflow.com/updates/gsap-becomes-free.
- **Firecrawl:** Its source repository describes the main app as AGPL-3.0 with some separately MIT-licensed components. See https://github.com/firecrawl/firecrawl/blob/main/README.md.

## C. Practical free/low-cost screening before offering a service

1. **Code license:** verify SPDX ID in the project's own license file and dependencies; distinguish core from enterprise/hosted features; assess GPL/AGPL obligations and attribution requirements for distribution or hosted access.
2. **Model fees:** free agent harnesses frequently require paid API credits, separate image/video models or provider subscriptions. Measure requests, prompts, context, media processing and storage.
3. **Hosting and operations:** confirm VM limits, per-request quotas, bandwidth, cold starts, database storage, backup, disaster recovery and monitoring costs. A no-price trial is not a perpetual free SLA.
4. **Rights and privacy:** verify dataset provenance, commercial media/stock/font rights, face/voice consent, personally identifiable data handling and model-provider retention.
5. **Reliability and security:** lock versions, audit dependencies, protect credentials, test permission boundaries, arrange rollback and incident response; disallow untrusted autonomous actions on production systems.
6. **User promise:** describe a tool as a candidate or demonstration until an ESER-owned installation, functional QA evidence, licensing signoff and delivery capacity are documented.
